TicketLoop build log

A FastAPI and Postgres backend for fixed-price ticket resale, built to stay correct when 100 buyers hit one ticket at once, when requests are retried, when webhooks arrive twice, and when the message broker goes down.

One page per finished module: what it built, the decisions behind it, and the questions an interviewer will ask.

Before an interview

  1. Say the project sentence above out loud, then each finished module's 30-second answer.
  2. Open each module's interview questions and answer before you reveal the answer.
  3. Rehearse the six hard problems. They are the part worth talking about for longest.
